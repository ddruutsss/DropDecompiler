iOS apps

        

          

            
Headers, Swift interfaces and report
Always on: Objective-C headers of every class (class-dump), Swift types with their properties, decoded plists, strings and interface files, entitlements, signing and a security report.

          

          
        

        
Android

        

          

            
Original names from mapping files
Always on: a ProGuard/R8 mapping.txt next to the app (or the one inside an .aab) restores the original class, method and field names. Kotlin names are restored from the app's metadata.
